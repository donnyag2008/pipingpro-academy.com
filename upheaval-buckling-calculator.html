Driving Force & Resistance — DNV-RP-F110
Effective axial force: S = −Heff = −(EA × α × ΔT − ν × P × Ai)
Soil passive resistance: q = γ′ × H × Do × f(H/D)
Buckle onset criterion: EI × wo × (π/L)⁴ ≤ S × (π/L)² − q
Required cover depth H from iterating the above for the project imperfection amplitude
DNV-RP-F110 Section 4 provides the upheaval buckling design criteria. The driving force is the compressive effective axial force from temperature and pressure. Soil resistance depends on burial depth, pipe diameter, and soil density.
