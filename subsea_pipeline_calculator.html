Design Criteria — DNV-ST-F101
Pressure containment: t1 = pd × D / (2 × fy × αU × γm × γSC + pd)
External collapse: (pe − pi) ≤ pc(t) / (γm × γSC)
Propagating buckle pressure: ppr = 35 × fy × (t/D)^2.5
On-bottom stability: FD + FI ≤ μ × (Ws − FL)
Free span natural frequency: fn = (π/2L²) × √(EI/me)
