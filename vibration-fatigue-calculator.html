API-618 Design Approach Guide

Approach 1 — Compressors rated below 150 BHP, final discharge < 150 psia, single-stage with ≤2 cylinders. Acoustical evaluation using standard analytical techniques.

Approach 2 — All compressors not covered by Approach 1 or 3. Full pulsation analysis using acoustical piping analog technique.

Approach 3 — Compressors with driver >500 BHP, or in critical/hazardous service. Full pulsation analysis + dynamic mechanical analysis of complete system.

Terminal Point Reference — What Qualifies as a Stop Point?

✓ Qualifies as terminal point (vibration stop):

1. Control valves / orifices with pressure drop ≥ 25% of average upstream line pressure — act as acoustic hard ends.

2. Blinds and all normally-closed valves (relief valves, N₂ purge valves, etc.).

3. Major process vessels (sufficient vapor volume per Eq. 1 above).

✗ Does NOT qualify (must be included in pulsation analysis):

Filters, knockout pots, intercoolers, aftercoolers, and vessels of similar size — unless verified to meet the volume requirement above.
