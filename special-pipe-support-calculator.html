Engineering disclaimer

This calculator is provided as a preliminary design and checking aid only. Results must not be used as the sole basis for construction, fabrication, or procurement without independent review and sign-off by a qualified Chartered or Licensed Structural/Mechanical Engineer.

  • BS 5950 was withdrawn on 30 March 2010 and superseded by BS EN 1993 (Eurocode 3). Its use on new UK/European projects requires explicit client or regulatory acceptance.
  • AISC 360 ASD and LRFD resistance values are approximated by proportional scaling from BS 5950 MOMENTS 50 tabulated data. A fully code-compliant AISC submission requires independent calculation of φMn (LRFD) or Mn/Ω (ASD) per AISC 360 Chapter F using section-specific compact/non-compact classification.
  • Weld checks use the same proportional scaling. Rigorous weld design must follow AISC 360 Chapter J (fillet weld: 0.6FEXX × 0.707 × throat) or BS EN 1993-1-8 as applicable.
  • Deflection limits (L/720 beam, H/300 column) are indicative. Project-specific limits from the applicable piping specification, client standard (e.g. Aramco SAES-L-310), or structural engineer of record shall govern.
  • The T-post configuration is not included in the original reference spreadsheet. The structural model (symmetric arms, fixed base column) is an engineering approximation and should be validated for each specific geometry.
  • No account is taken of dynamic loads, vibration, wind, seismic, thermal expansion forces, or friction effects from pipe movement. These must be assessed separately per the applicable project loading specification.
  • Section properties are based on DIN/European sections (SSMS database). Verify section availability and dimensional equivalence before specifying on projects using ASTM/AISC sections.
  • This tool has not been independently verified or validated. It shall not be stamped, sealed, or submitted as a formal engineering calculation without full independent review.
