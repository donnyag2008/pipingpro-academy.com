Stress Critical Line Selector

Classifies process lines into C1 / C2 / C3 / NC for the level of flexibility and stress analysis required \u2014 the twelve-criterion EPC Line Designation Table screen, with the size-vs-temperature Criticality Requirement Chart. Assess a single line, or batch-process an entire LDT.

Screening recommendation, not a verdict. Output is an engineering screening of the level of analysis required \u2014 it does not replace the project's stress-critical line list procedure or the responsible stress engineer's judgement. Thresholds reproduce a representative EPC LDT method; verify against your own project specification.

Method

Every applicable check returns a candidate class; the line takes the most onerous result. A line whose size cell is non-numeric (a section-break / continuation row) is flagged LDT? for manual review. The fluid-service code is read from characters 10\u201312 of the line number; the From / To tags identify connected equipment by prefix.

1 \u00b7 Geometry & condition checks (every line)

Cold size: NPD < 2″ \u2192 NC  |  < 16″ \u2192 C3  |  < 24″ \u2192 C2  |  \u2265 24″ \u2192 C1
Low temperature: (T \u2264 \u2212101 \u00b0C & \u2265 2″) or (T \u2264 \u221250 \u00b0C & \u2265 6″) \u2192 C2
High temperature: (T \u2265 280 \u00b0C & \u2265 2″) or (T \u2265 200 \u00b0C & \u2265 6″) \u2192 C1
Two-phase flow (0 < vapour% < 100): > 6″ \u2192 C1  |  > 3″ \u2192 C2  |  \u2265 2″ \u2192 C3

2 \u00b7 Equipment-connectivity checks (line must touch the tag)

Boiler (B-): \u2265 4″ \u2192 C1
Compressor (C-): HC any size \u2192 C1  |  gas svc > 4″ \u2192 C1, > 2″ \u2192 C2
Heater / furnace (H-): HC \u2265 4″ \u2192 C1
Pump (P-, liquid): process svc > 3″ \u2192 C1  |  water svc > 4″ \u2192 C1
Reactor (R-): HC > 3″ \u2192 C1
Gas turbine (CGT-): fuel gas > 4″ \u2192 C2
Steam turbine (ST-): steam svc > 3″ \u2192 C1
Air cooler (listed E- tags): HC > 4″ \u2192 C1, > 3″ \u2192 C2
Roll-up note. Following the source LDT, only the geometry checks (size, low-/high-temp) and two-phase flow escalate the final class to C2 / C3. A standalone equipment C2 (compressor, gas-turbine or air-cooler at the C2 threshold) is shown in the breakdown but does not by itself raise the final class above NC. The per-check table always shows the full picture, so the engineer can override by judgement.

3 \u00b7 Criticality Requirement Chart

The project's graphical size-vs-temperature screen, reproduced from the chart data. Two stepped boundaries split the field into three zones \u2014 C3 (inner: small bore, moderate temperature), C2 (middle), and C1 (outer: large bore and/or extreme temperature). Temperature is in \u00b0F to match the source chart; design temperatures entered in \u00b0C are converted automatically when a line is plotted.
