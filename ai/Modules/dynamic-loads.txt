## DYNAMIC AND SPECIAL LOADS

### PSV and Rupture Disc Reaction Forces
The load when a PSV opens or rupture disc ruptures must ALWAYS be considered.

PSV Reaction (including impact factor of 2):
  F = 0.6 x (K0 + 1) x A x P

Rupture Disc Reaction (including impact factor of 2):
  F = 0.378 x (K0 + 1) x A x P

Where:
  K0 = Cp/Cv (ratio of specific heats)
  A = Orifice Area
  P = Absolute Pressure
  (All in consistent units)

### Slug Flow and Water Hammer
Slug flow = bolus of liquid flowing along a pipe. Similar effect from sudden valve closure/opening (water hammer).

Static force on a 90 deg elbow due to slug flow (with 2x impact factor):
  F = 2.8 x rho x A x V^2 / g

Where:
  F = Force
  rho = Density of fluid
  A = Pipe internal area
  V = Fluid velocity
  g = Gravitational constant
  (All in consistent units)

Force direction: 45 deg to direction of flow into the elbow, radially outward from the elbow centre.

Impact of slug or wave front onto an elbow can be EXCESSIVE — consider alternative designs where possible.

### Seismic
- Horizontal seismic coefficient typically h = 0.34 (project-specific — verify from UBC Zone or local code)
- Dynamic response spectrum analysis only if equipment or piping arrangement appears unusually sensitive to vibration
- Alternatively, response spectrum method may be used
- No clearly established limit in codes for earthquake loads on equipment nozzles — if loads exceed allowable, seek approval from Mechanical group on individual basis

### Wind Loads
- Consider for large lines in exposed condition (typically >24" OD above 9m)
- Wind load: F = design wind pressure x Cf x exposed area
- Cf = 0.7 for cylindrical pipes
- Include effect on stresses AND loads on restraints/nozzles
- If wind causes excessive nozzle loads, consider actual site conditions, elevation, local obstructions, and frequency of design wind speed

### Wind Deflection of Columns and Structures
Initial calculation value: L/200 where L = elevation of column/structure.
Confirm with vessel/structural group for specific column as soon as possible.

### High Pressure Drop at Control Valves
Where high pressure drop exists across control valves:
- Expect high fluid velocities and extreme noise levels
- Downstream piping shall be analysed to prevent excessive vibration and noise
- Size and configuration downstream of valve are critical

### Settlement
- Consider differential settlement between supports and equipment
- May use adjustable supports where standard maintenance is assured
- May require pipe flexibility and spring supports
- Typical initial values: Gross settlement <= 25mm (takes place before pipe hook-up due to equipment mass)
- Differential settlement between foundations: 13mm as design figure
- Tankage: design for 25mm (low mass = little initial settlement until filled)

### Large Displacement Warning
Where large thermal or other displacements exceed 50mm:
- Check pipe does not impact adjacent lines, equipment, or structure
- Check supports do not run off their structural base
