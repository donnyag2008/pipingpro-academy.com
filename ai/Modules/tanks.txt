## EQUIPMENT-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE: STORAGE TANKS

## LOAD CASES: STORAGE TANK
- Design condition
- Settlement (residual after hydrostatic test)
- Bulging effect
- Hydrostatic pressure test of insulated piping with blocked spring support (if required)

## TANK NOZZLE BOUNDARY CONDITIONS
Lines shall be analysed with the following boundary conditions at tank nozzles (furnished by tank supplier):
- Stiffness coefficients of nozzle for axial load, longitudinal moment and circumferential moment
- Radial movement of nozzle due to maximum service temperature and full of product
- Rotation of nozzle due to maximum service temperature and full of product

Allowable loads shall be according to vendor allowable or applicable code.
