## EQUIPMENT-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE: PRESSURE VESSELS

When user has a line connected to a pressure vessel:

1. GET VESSEL DRAWING from Mechanical Group. Calculate initial movement for EACH nozzle using PPA calculator, including the first support after the nozzle.

2. NOZZLES AT HIGH ELEVATIONS — CRITICAL: For nozzles where pipe goes DOWN immediately after leaving the nozzle, the first support must be as CLOSE AS POSSIBLE to the nozzle. Purpose: reduce load imposed at the nozzle.

3. FIRST SUPPORT TEMPERATURE: First support after vessel nozzle, welded to vessel shell, SHALL use SHELL TEMPERATURE (not pipe temperature). This synchronises nozzle movement and support movement — correctly represents pipe movement, thermal and sustained stress.

4. OPERATING TEMPERATURE of equipment shall be used to evaluate movements at equipment nozzles and pipe supports attached to the vessel.

5. VERTICAL PIPE GUIDE SPACING: Number of guides on vertical runs per PPA pipe support span calculator for vertical pipes.

## LOAD CASES: VERTICAL VESSEL (Column, Tower)
- Design condition
- Start-up condition
- Steam-out condition (Vessel hot, Line cold)
- Wind effect (if necessary)
- Earthquake effect (if necessary)

## LOAD CASES: HORIZONTAL VESSEL (Exchanger, Drum)
- Design condition
- Design condition with by-pass closed (must consider temperature decay in piping)
- Earthquake effect
- Hydrostatic pressure test (if required)

## LOAD CASES: REACTOR
- Design condition
- Start-up condition
- Regeneration condition
- Wind effect (if necessary)
- Earthquake effect
- Hydrostatic pressure test of insulated piping with blocked spring support (if required)
