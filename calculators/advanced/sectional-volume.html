Engineering Notes
Average End-Area Method
V = Σ [ (Aᵢ + Aᵢ₊₁)/2 × Lᵢ ]
A = cross-section areas at consecutive stations · L = distance between them
The workhorse method for trench and excavation quantities; works for any spacing. Slightly over-estimates on concave runs — the prismoidal method corrects this.
Prismoidal (Simpson's 1/3) Method
V = (d/3) × [ A₀ + 4(A₁+A₃+…) + 2(A₂+A₄+…) + Aₙ ]
d = constant spacing · requires an odd number of sections
More accurate where area varies smoothly. Requires equal spacing and an odd number of cross-sections; otherwise the calculator falls back to average end-area.
Trapezoidal Trench Area
A = (b + s·h) · h (symmetric side slopes)
b = bottom width · h = depth · s = side slope (H:1V) · top width = b + 2sh
Side-slope selection follows the soil's safe angle and the governing excavation safety code. Vertical faces (s = 0) require shoring or trench boxes.
Swell / Bulking Factor
Loose = in-situ (bank) volume × (1 + swell%)
1 m³ = 1.30795 yd³ · 1 yd³ = 27 ft³
Indicative bank → loose swell: sand 10–15%, common earth 20–30%, clay 30–40%, rock 50%+. Use project-specific factors for haulage and disposal.
