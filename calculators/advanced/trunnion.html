Local Stress at Trunnion-to-Pipe Junction
Trunnion shear stress: τ = FV / (π × dt × tt)
Bending stress in trunnion: σb = M / Zt  where M = FH × Ht
Junction weld stress: σw = √(σb² + τ²) ≤ 0.6 × Sy
Local shell stress (WRC 107): σlocal = K × P × ro / tp ≤ 1.5 × Sm
Trunnion supports are commonly used on vertical vessels and large-bore piping in O&G plants. The critical checks are the junction weld between trunnion and pipe (in shear and bending) and the local shell stress on the run pipe. For hot piping, the trunnion must allow axial thermal movement — slotted holes or PTFE slide plates are specified.
