Cathodic Protection Sizing

Buried-pipeline CP sizing aligned to SAES-X-400. The tool applies the standard's selection rule (ICCP is the default; galvanic standalone is the narrow exception), Table 4 current densities, Table 7 galvanic consumption rates, and the §7.5 circuit-resistance check.

Scope: SAES-X-400 governs buried pipelines outside plant facilities. Plant external surfaces and buried plant piping fall under SAES-X-600; marine/immersed under X-300; well casings under X-700. The non-pipeline modes below use the same engine but are outside X-400 — confirm densities/criteria against the applicable standard.
