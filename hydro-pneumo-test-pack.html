    Test Pressure Formulae — ASME B31.3
    Hydrostatic test pressure: PT = 1.5 × Pdesign × ST/Sh  [psig]
    ST = allowable stress at test temperature
    Sh = allowable stress at design temperature

    Pneumatic test pressure: PT = 1.1 × Pdesign × ST/Sh

    Minimum hold time: 10 minutes (B31.3 345.2.2) — then reduce to design pressure for inspection
    Typical field practice: 30–60 minutes at test pressure before inspection walk-down
    Pneumatic testing is inherently more hazardous than hydrostatic — stored energy at test pressure is orders of magnitude higher. B31.3 requires documented hazard assessment before pneumatic testing is permitted. Safety relief valve at 1.1 × test pressure is mandatory. Minimum personnel exclusion zone during pressure hold.
