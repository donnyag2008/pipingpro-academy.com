Stress Analysis — API RP 1102
Soil dead load stress: σE = Kb × Bd × γ × H / (Do × t)  [psi]
Live load stress (wheel): σL = CL × P / (Do × t)  [psi]
Hoop stress (internal pressure): σh = Pd × Di / (2t)  [psi]
Combined longitudinal stress: σx = σE + σL + ν × σh
Equivalent stress (von Mises): σeq = √(σh² − σhσx + σx²) ≤ 0.9 × SMYS
