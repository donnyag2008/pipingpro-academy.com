ASME B31.3-2020 · Process Piping — Clause 304.1.2 Straight Pipe Under Internal Pressure
tm = t + c     [Eq. (2)]
t = P·D / [ 2·(S·E·W + P·Y) ]     [Eq. (3a)]

c = sum of mechanical allowances (thread/groove depth) plus corrosion and erosion allowances (mm)
D = outside diameter of pipe, mm (as listed in standards or as measured)
E = quality factor from Table A-1A or A-1B — 1.00 for seamless
P = internal design gage pressure, MPa
S = stress value for material from Table A-1 or A-1M at design temperature, MPa
t = pressure design thickness (calculated), mm
tm = minimum required thickness including mechanical, corrosion and erosion allowances, mm
T = pipe wall thickness (measured or minimum per purchase specification), mm — must be ≥ tm
W = weld joint strength reduction factor per para. 302.3.5(e) — see table below
Y = coefficient from Table 304.1.1, valid for t < D/6 — see table below

Mill tolerance: tordered = (t + c) / (1 − mill_tol/100)   — round up to next commercial schedule
For t ≥ D/6 or P/SE > 0.385, special consideration of factors such as theory of failure, fatigue, and thermal stress is required per para. 304.1.2(b). This calculator covers only the t < D/6 case.

ASME B31.3-2020 · Table 302.3.5 — Weld Joint Strength Reduction Factor W
W applies to longitudinal welds at elevated temperature to account for creep strength reduction of the weld joint relative to the base metal.

Carbon Steel: W = 1.0 for all temperatures up to 760°C (1,400°F)
CrMo steels (½Cr–½Mo through 9Cr–1Mo):
   W = 1.00 at ≤427°C (800°F)  ·  0.95 at 454°C  ·  0.91 at 482°C  ·  0.86 at 510°C  ·  0.82 at 538°C  ·  0.77 at 566°C  ·  0.73 at 593°C
CSEF steels (N+T) — grades 91, 92, 911, 122, 23:
   W = 1.00 at ≤510°C  ·  0.95 at 538°C  ·  0.91 at 566°C  ·  0.86 at 593°C  ·  0.82 at 621°C
Austenitic SS grade 3xx and N088xx nickel alloys:
   W = 1.00 at ≤510°C  ·  0.95 at 538°C  ·  0.91 at 566°C  ·  0.86 at 593°C  ·  0.82 at 621°C  ·  0.77 at 649°C
W = 1.0 for seamless pipe at all temperatures (no longitudinal weld). Linear interpolation is permitted for intermediate temperatures. See full Table 302.3.5 for CSEF subcritical PWHT and autogenous weld cases.
ASME B31.4-2019 · Liquid Pipelines — Clause 403.2.1 Criteria
tn ≥ t + A

US Customary:   t = Pi·D / (2·S)
SI Units:            t = Pi·D / (20·S)

A = sum of allowances for threading, grooving, corrosion and erosion (para. 403.2.2–403.2.4), mm (in.)
D = outside diameter of pipe, in. (mm)
Pi = internal design gage pressure, psi (bar)
S = applicable allowable stress value, psi (MPa), determined by: S = F × E × Sy
   E = weld joint factor per Table 403.2.1-1 — 1.00 for seamless and SAW
   F = design factor — shall not be greater than 0.72 per this Code
   Sy = specified minimum yield strength, psi (MPa)
t = pressure design wall thickness, in. (mm)
tn = nominal wall thickness satisfying requirements for pressure and allowances, in. (mm)

⚠️ No temperature derating factor in B31.4 Eq. 403.2.1. The SI factor of 20 (vs. 2 in US Customary) accounts for the unit conversion: 1 bar = 0.1 MPa, hence 2S × 10 = 20S.

Note: B31.4 para. 403.2.3 states corrosion allowance is not required if pipe is protected per Chapter VIII. Where required, it is included in A.
B31.4 design factors reflect the underthickness tolerances and maximum allowable imperfection depths in the applicable pipe specifications — no additional mill tolerance deduction is required beyond selecting a nominal schedule wall ≥ tn.

ASME B31.8-2003 · Gas Transmission — Para. 841.11 Steel Pipe Design Formula
P = 2·S·t·F·E·T / D     (design pressure form)
t = P·D / (2·S·F·E·T)     (wall thickness form)

D = nominal outside diameter of pipe, in.
E = longitudinal joint factor from Table 841.115A
F = design factor from Table 841.114A (see table below)
P = design pressure, psig — see also para. 841.111 for limitations
S = specified minimum yield strength (SMYS), psi — per pipe purchase specification or paras. 817.13(h) and 841.112
T = temperature derating factor from Table 841.116A (see table below)
t = nominal wall thickness, in.

Add corrosion allowance separately after t is calculated. Apply mill tolerance (12.5% under per API 5L) to arrive at specified nominal wall.
B31.8 uses the hoop stress design formula in US Customary units natively. S is SMYS — not a temperature-derated allowable stress. The design factor F already incorporates underthickness tolerance allowances from the approved pipe specifications.
