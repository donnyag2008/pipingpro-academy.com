t = P · D / [ 2 · (S · E · W + P · Y) ]
t = required minimum wall thickness (mm)  |  P = design pressure (barg)  |  D = outside diameter (mm)
S = allowable stress at design temp (MPa)  |  E = longitudinal joint factor  |  W = weld strength reduction factor  |  Y = temp coefficient

ℹ️ B31.3 Eq. 304.1.2(a): t = P·D / [2·(S·E·W + P·Y)]  +  CA + c₂
Final specified thickness = tcalc / (1 − MT/100) rounded up to next commercial schedule.
3 — ASME B31.4 Material & Code Factors
ℹ️ B31.4 Eq. 403.2.1: t = P·D / (2·S·F·E·T) + CA
S = SMYS of pipe material. Final thickness includes mill tolerance per API 5L / ASME B36.10.
3 — ASME B31.8 Material & Code Factors
ℹ️ B31.8 Eq. 841.1.1: t = P·D / (2·S·F·E·T·L) + CA
S = SMYS. Hoop stress limited to F·E·T·S. Note: B31.8 uses SMYS (not allowable stress from tables).
