Hot Insulation Material — Select Type
Most common
Mineral Wool (Rock Wool)
Up to 650°C
Pre-formed pipe sections or mattresses. k ≈ 0.033–0.046 W/m·K at 50°C. Standard for process piping 120–550°C in O&G plants. ASTM C547 / BS EN 14303. Aluminium cladding jacket.
High temp
Calcium Silicate
Up to 650°C (singles) / 760°C dual layer
Rigid pre-formed sections. Excellent mechanical strength, resistant to vibration and traffic. k ≈ 0.055–0.085 W/m·K. Preferred for steam headers, high-temp process lines, where personnel may stand. ASTM C533.
Premium
Aerogel Blanket
–270°C to +650°C
Pyrogel / Spaceloft. k ≈ 0.015–0.020 W/m·K — 2–5× better than mineral wool. Thin profile, hydrophobic. Used where space is constrained (offshore platforms, pipe racks). ASTM C1728. ~3–5× cost premium.
High temp
Expanded Perlite
Up to 820°C
Rigid pre-formed sections, hydrophobic grades available. k ≈ 0.065–0.10 W/m·K. High mechanical strength. Used on furnace piping, high-temp steam. ASTM C610. Common in refinery applications.
Dual-use
Cellular Glass
–268°C to +482°C
Closed-cell, impermeable to moisture, non-combustible, excellent compressive strength. k ≈ 0.040–0.055 W/m·K. Used where CUI risk is high or below-grade piping. ASTM C552.
Light duty
Glass Wool (Fibreglass)
Up to 300°C
Lightweight blanket or pre-formed sections. k ≈ 0.030–0.040 W/m·K. Economical for lower-temperature utility lines (hot water, LP steam, condensate). ASTM C547 / ASTM C553.
Mineral Wool selected — standard for O&G process piping 120–550°C with aluminium cladding jacket.

Paint Coverage Formula
External surface area: A = π × OD × L  [m²]
Theoretical coverage: Cth = 1000 × Vs / DFT  [m²/L]
Vs = volume solids fraction (0–1)  |  DFT = dry film thickness (µm)
Practical coverage: Cpr = Cth × application efficiency
Volume per coat: Vol = A / Cpr  [litres]
With wastage: Volgross = Vol × (1 + waste factor)
Insulation Volume Formula
Insulation OD: Dins = OD + 2×tins
Net insulation volume: V = π/4 × (Dins² − OD²) × L  [m³]
Cladding area: Aclad = π × Dins × L  [m²] — outer surface of insulation
