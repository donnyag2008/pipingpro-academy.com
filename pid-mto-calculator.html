How Area Extraction Works
Step 1 — Title Block Read: AI scans the drawing title block and extracts Area/Unit, Drawing Number, Title, and Revision before you confirm adding to queue.
Step 2 — MTO Extraction: Full AI analysis extracts all pipe lines, valves, instruments, equipment, fittings, and special items, each tagged with the drawing number and area.
Step 3 — Aggregation: By Area view sums all quantities by area. The drawing number from the title block is used as the source reference — not the filename.
Area is derived from the Unit/Area field in the P&ID title block (e.g. "Unit 100 — Inlet Separation", "Area 2A", "Compression Train"). You can override the AI-read area in the modal before adding to queue. Duplicate line numbers or instrument tags appearing across drawings in the same area are flagged for boundary verification.
