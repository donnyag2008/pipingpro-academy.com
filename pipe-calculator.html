Formula Basis — ASME B36.10 / Nayyar 7th Edition
Pipe Metal Area: A = π/4 × (OD² − ID²)  [in²]
Pipe Weight (steel): wpipe = A × 12 × 0.2833  [lb/ft]  (ρsteel = 0.2833 lb/in³)
Fluid Weight: wfluid = π/4 × ID² × 12 × (ρfluid/1728)  [lb/ft]
Moment of Inertia: I = π/64 × (OD⁴ − ID⁴)  [in⁴]
Section Modulus: Z = I / (OD/2) = π/32 × (OD⁴ − ID⁴) / OD  [in³]
Radius of Gyration: r = √(I/A)  [in]
Wall thickness and OD values per ASME B36.10M (carbon steel) and B36.19M (stainless steel). Density 0.2833 lb/in³ = 490 lb/ft³ for carbon and low-alloy steel.
