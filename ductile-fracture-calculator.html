3 — EMC²PDFA Two-Approach Methodology (SAES-L-131 §5.1.3)
Per Fadhili GIP KBR project practice and SAES-L-131 §5.1.3, when H₂S is present in the gas composition, the following two-approach method is required — because current EMC²PDFA (GASDECOM EOS) cannot process H₂S directly.
1
Gas Composition Screening SAES-L-131 §5.1.3
Confirm gas is rich/sour — C₁ < 90 mol%, or H₂S ≥ 1 mol%, or significant CO₂/NGL. BCL formula not applicable. Proceed to two-approach BTCM.
2
Approach 1 — Approximate (Excluding H₂S) EMC²PDFA + GASDECOM
Modify gas composition to exclude H₂S component. Redistribute remaining components to 100 mol%. Run EMC²PDFA with GASDECOM EOS — use original BTC, Leis, or Wilkowski method. This gives a conservative approximate CVN. This calculator provides the BCL formula result as Approach 1 approximation below.
3
Approach 2 — Rigorous (Full Composition) REFPROP + EMC²PDFA REQUIRED
Use NIST REFPROP to generate gas decompression curve with full composition including H₂S and CO₂. Export decompression curve data. Import into EMC²PDFA as user-defined decompression curve input. Run full two-curve analysis to determine minimum CVN. This is the definitive result submitted to Aramco.
4
CVN Specification API 5L Annex G
Required CVN = max(Approach 1, Approach 2, API 5L Table 8 minimum, ASME B31.8 §841.1.2(c) minimum of 74 ft-lb / 100 J). Specify on line pipe material requisition and incorporate into DCPR/ITPR.
5
DWTT Consideration EMC²PDFA Option
For X70 / X80 pipe or steels exhibiting separations on fracture surface — consider DWTT (Drop Weight Tear Test) energy instead of CVN. DWTT is independent of grade and fabrication effects. EMC²PDFA accepts both PN-DWTT and static-precracked DWTT inputs. Discuss with Aramco materials team.
4 — Three EMC²PDFA CVN Methods — Explanation
Original BTC Equation
Cv = 0.0147 × σH² × (Dt/2)^0.284
Classic Battelle formula. Aramco SAES-L-131 §5.1.1 baseline. Conservative for modern steels. Used for Approach 1 approximation in this calculator.
Leis Modification
Cv_Leis = Cv_BTC × f(σf)
σf = flow stress = (SMYS+UTS)/2. Accounts for real material toughness more accurately. Less conservative than BTC for modern high-toughness steels. Available in EMC²PDFA.
Wilkowski Modification
Grade-specific statistical correction
Statistical correction factors by pipe grade (X52–X80). Critical for modern X65 and above — fabrication effects can significantly alter Charpy energy. Required for rising-shelf steels with separations.

Battelle Two-Curve Method (BTCM)
Gas decompression wave speed (Battelle):
VD = (1 + B) × Vs — where Vs = sonic velocity and B is backfill/diameter correction

Fracture propagation speed (Charpy-based):
VF = C1 × [σh/σf − C2]

Arrest condition: VD > VF at all pressures down to arrest

Required Charpy (BCL formula, lean gas):
CVN = 2.382 × 10⁻⁴ × σh^1.5 × D^0.5 × Rt  [J]  or equivalent ft-lb
SAES-L-131 Table 1 specifies which method applies by MAOP, diameter, and gas composition. For rich gas (C₂+ > 10 mol%) or CO₂ > 10%, GASDECOM decompression analysis is required rather than Battelle ideal gas assumption.
