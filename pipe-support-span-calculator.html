Design Basis — M.W. Kellogg Formulae
1. Stress Method — Maximum bending stress (simply supported, UDL)
σ = w × L² × 12 / (8 × Z)   [psi]
Rearranged for L:   L = √( 8 × σallow × Z / (w × 12) )   [ft]
σ = bending stress (psi) | w = total unit weight (lb/ft) | L = span (ft) | Z = section modulus (in³)
2. Deflection Method — Maximum midspan deflection (simply supported, UDL)
δ = 22.5 × w × L⁴ / (E × I)   [inches]
Rearranged for L:   L = ⁴√( δallow × E × I / 22.5 × w )   [ft]
δ = deflection (in) | E = modulus of elasticity (psi) | I = moment of inertia (in⁴) | constant 22.5 = 5×12⁴/384×12 incorporates unit conversion ft→in
3. Natural Frequency of Pipe Span (simply supported, UDL)
fₙ = (π²/2L²) × √(E × I × g / (w/12))   [cps]   ... simplified form:
fₙ ≈ 3.573 / L² × √(E × I / (w/12))   [cps, L in ft, w in lb/ft]
g = 386.4 in/s² | The constant 3.573 = π²/(2×12²) × √(386.4) incorporating unit conversions
The governing span is the lesser of Lstress and Ldeflection. In practice the deflection criterion is usually more restrictive for smaller pipe and the stress criterion governs for larger bore pipe.
Deflection Limits — M.W. Kellogg / Industry Practice
Service Recommended deflection limit Natural frequency target Notes
Average process piping ½" to 1" ≥ 4 cps M.W. Kellogg practical limit
Power piping ⅛" (0.125") ≥ 4 cps More conservative; stiff system preferred
Reciprocating compressor piping Project-specific ≥ 8 cps (or per API-618 MNFP) Higher frequency target to avoid pulsation resonance
Lines prone to slug flow / two-phase ¼" or less ≥ 6 cps Slug impact forces amplified at large deflections
Layout Reduction Factors — Rule of Thumb
Layout Factor Description
Straight / Continuous run 1.00 No direction change. Full table span applies.
L-bend (one direction change) 0.75 Pipe changes direction once after the last support. Max span = 0.75 × L.
Z-bend / U-bend (two direction changes) 0.65 Pipe changes direction twice (Z-bend, U-bend, offset). Max span = 0.65 × L.
These factors are rule-of-thumb values per M.W. Kellogg and general industry practice. Individual project piping specifications may specify different values. Always confirm with your project standard.
Reference
Primary formula source: Design of Piping Systems, The M. W. Kellogg Company, Revised Second Edition, 1965.
Supporting codes: ASME B31.3 (Process Piping), ASME B31.1 (Power Piping), ASME B36.10 (Pipe dimensions).
Allowable stress: Sh values from ASME B31.3 Table A-1 at the pipe's maximum operating temperature.
Note: The formula assumes simply-supported end conditions. For clamped or guided spans, natural frequency is higher (×1.6 correction factor per Truong / API-618), but the stress and deflection formulas remain conservative.
