2.What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data — your name and email address, and the password used to secure your account.
  • Subscription and payment data — your plan, billing history, and the country associated with your payment method. Card details are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Eligibility data — where you apply for the Student tier, information you provide to verify student status (such as a student email address or identification).
  • Usage data — information about how you use the Service, such as pages and calculators accessed, and basic technical data such as browser type and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Content you submit to AI tools — where you use AI-assisted features (such as drawing or document analysis), the files and inputs you upload for processing.
  • Communications — the content of messages you send us.

The Service uses cookies and similar technologies, including browser local storage, to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, secure the Service, and understand how it is used. We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the Service. After your account is closed, we retain data only as required to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at donny.bachtiar@pipingpro-academy.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. If you are in the UK or EU and have concerns we have not resolved, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

10.Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

The Service is intended for professionals and students aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
