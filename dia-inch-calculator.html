Engineering Notes
Dia-Inch (Diameter-Inch)
Dia-Inch = Σ ( NPS × number of welds )
One butt weld of an NPS-n pipe = n dia-inches · e.g. 8 welds on 6" pipe = 48 DI
The standard unit for estimating fabrication, welding and NDE effort. It scales weld count by pipe size. Weld type (butt, socket, branch) is recorded for the breakdown only and does not change the DI value.
Man-Hours by Zone Productivity
Man-hours = Dia-Inch × productivity (mh / DI)
Pipe rack ≈ 0.8–1.2 mh/DI · Equipment area ≈ 1.3–2.0 mh/DI
Splitting scope by location is where most estimates go soft. Rack welding is repetitive with good access; equipment-area welding is congested and tie-in heavy, so it runs materially slower per dia-inch.
Labour Cost & Change-Order Check
Labour cost = man-hours × all-in rate (cost / hr)
All-in rate = welder + fitter + helper + overhead
As Owner's Engineer, build the weld scope independently, compute DI → man-hours → cost, and benchmark against the contractor's claim. A large gap is the prompt to interrogate the claimed weld count, productivity or rate.
