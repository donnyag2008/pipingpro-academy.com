Thermal Expansion — ASME B31.3
Thermal expansion: ΔL = α × L × ΔT  [in]
α = coefficient of thermal expansion (in/in/°F)
Carbon steel: α ≈ 6.5×10⁻⁶ /°F  |  Stainless 316: α ≈ 9.9×10⁻⁶ /°F

Guided Cantilever — Loop leg length:
H = √(3 × E × Do × ΔL / SA)  [in]  → convert to feet (/12)

Allowable displacement stress range (B31.3 Eq. 1a):
SA = f × (1.25Sc + 0.25Sh)  [psi]
f = stress range reduction factor (1.0 for ≤7000 cycles)
