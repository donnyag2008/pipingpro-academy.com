Caesar II Stress Analysis Methodology
Sustained stress (SUS): SL = P×D/(4t) + 0.75×i×MA/Z ≤ Sh
Displacement stress (EXP): SE = √(Sb² + 4St²) ≤ SA
Occasional stress (OCC): SL + Socc ≤ 1.33Sh (B31.3)
SA = f × (1.25Sc + 0.25Sh) — allowable displacement stress range
SIF (i) = stress intensification factor — elbows, tees, reducers per Appendix D
Caesar II models piping systems as beam elements. Key inputs: pipe geometry, material properties, operating conditions, support types and gaps. Load cases must cover all SUS, EXP, OCC, and HYD combinations. Modelling errors — missing spring rates, wrong friction, incorrect anchor stiffness — are the most common sources of analysis errors.
