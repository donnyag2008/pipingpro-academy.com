Cold & Hot Allowable Stress

In ASME B31.3 displacement-stress evaluation, the cold allowable Sc is the basic allowable stress at the minimum metal temperature (taken here at ambient, 21 °C), and the hot allowable Sh is the basic allowable stress at the maximum metal (design) temperature. Both come from Appendix A, Table A-1.

SA = f ( 1.25 Sc + 0.25 Sh )
SA = allowable displacement stress range  ·  f = stress-range reduction factor (1.0 for ≤ 7 000 cycles)
Sc is fixed at ambient; Sh, α and E all fall with temperature, so the datasheet interpolates them linearly to your entered design temperature.
Thermal Expansion & Modulus

The mean coefficient of thermal expansion α (Table C-1) is referenced to 21 °C and is used to compute free thermal growth ΔL = α · L · ΔT. The modulus of elasticity E (Table C-6) is the cold modulus reduced for temperature, and sets the stiffness that converts imposed displacement into stress.

Austenitic stainless steels expand roughly 40 % more than carbon steel and have a lower modulus — both increase displacement stress, which is why material choice drives flexibility design.
Data Basis & Verification Status

Verified — A106 Gr.B, TP304, TP316L and Duplex 2205 use the Sc / Sh and modulus values already validated in the PipingPro wall-thickness and expansion datasets.

Indicative — A333 Gr.6, A335 P11 and P22 use standard reference values; the structure and interpolation are correct, but confirm the Sh allowables and Cr-Mo creep-range figures against your governing B31.3 edition. The α values across all materials are from the Table C-1 family curves and should be cross-checked against your CAESAR II material library.

Sources: ASME B31.3 Appendix A (allowable stress), Table C-1 (thermal expansion), Table C-6 (modulus of elasticity).
