Engineering Notes
PEQ Method per ASME B16.5 / EN 1591 principles · Gasket G from ASME B16.20 (spiral wound) / B16.21 (flat/raised face) / B16.20 (ring joint).
P16.5 ratings interpolated for Group 1.1 carbon steel. For other material groups adjust P16.5 accordingly.
For preliminary engineering use only. Formal flange integrity analysis per ASME VIII App.2 / EN 1591-1 required for safety-critical applications.
Pressure Equivalent (PEQ) Method
Peq = Pdes + PEQ-M + PEQ-F
PEQ-M = (16/π) · Mr / G³  ·  PEQ-F = (4/π) · Fa / G²
Mr = √(Mb1² + Mb2²)  ·  G = gasket mean diameter
External bending moment and axial force are converted into an equivalent pressure and added to design pressure. The flange is leak-tight when the total equivalent pressure stays within the ASME B16.5 pressure-temperature rating.
Acceptance Check
Peq ≤ P16.5 (allowable at temperature)
Utilisation = Peq / P16.5 × 100%
≤ 85% — comfortable margin · 85–100% — marginal, recheck under upset/hydrotest · > 100% — fails; re-route piping, add flexibility, or upgrade the flange class.
Gasket Mean Diameter G
G from ASME B16.20 / B16.21 tables
Spiral wound (B16.20) · Flat / raised face (B16.21) · Ring joint (B16.20)
G is auto-populated by NPS, class and gasket type, and can be overridden from the actual gasket datasheet. For spiral wound gaskets, G is taken at the inner winding seating diameter.

Pressure Equivalent (PEQ) Method — ASME B16.5
Equivalent pressure: Peq = P + (16×M)/(π×G³) + (4×F)/(π×G²)  [psi]
P = internal pressure (psig)
M = bending moment at flange (in·lb)
F = axial force (lb) — tension positive
G = mean gasket diameter (in) — from ASME B16.20/B16.21

Adequacy check: Peq ≤ Prating at operating temperature
The PEQ method converts all loads to an equivalent pressure and checks against the ASME B16.5 pressure-temperature rating. It is conservative but widely accepted for most process applications. More rigorous methods (per ASME Section VIII Div.2 App.2) are needed for fatigue-critical or very high-pressure flanges.
